# Amwaj Islands Bahrain — The Complete 2026 Guide

Amwaj Islands is Bahrain's premier beachfront destination — a cluster of man-made islands off the northeastern coast near Muharraq. Known for their crystal-clear lagoons, upscale residential communities, and vibrant dining scene, Amwaj has become the go-to destination for both residents and visitors seeking beach days, waterfront dining, and island living.

Whether you're looking for a rooftop dinner with lagoon views, a day at the beach club, or a weekend escape in Bahrain, Amwaj Islands delivers.

## Getting to Amwaj Islands

Amwaj Islands sits just off the coast of Muharraq, approximately 15 minutes from Bahrain International Airport and 20 minutes from Manama's city centre. The islands are connected to the mainland by a short causeway, making them easily accessible by car or taxi.

**By car:** Take the North Muharraq causeway and follow signs to Amwaj Islands. Free parking is available throughout the island complex.
**By taxi:** A typical metered ride from Manama costs BHD 2–4.
**By ride-share:** Uber and Talab operate throughout Bahrain with pickup usually within 5 minutes.

## Best Restaurants in Amwaj Islands

Amwaj Islands has developed into one of Bahrain's most diverse dining destinations, with options ranging from casual beachside eateries to upscale waterfront fine dining.

### The Art Hotel & Resort Dining

The Art Hotel & Resort is a landmark on Amwaj Islands, housing several distinct dining venues:

- **Fusions** — Asian fusion cuisine with lagoon views. Known for its weekend brunch and sushi selection. Open daily from 12pm.
- **Leaf** — Mediterranean and healthy eating options. Popular for breakfast and light lunches overlooking the pool.
- **Latitude** — International buffet dining with live cooking stations. Popular for Friday brunch.

### Waterfront Dining

Several standalone restaurants line the Amwaj lagoon walk:

- **Cavo Rooftop** — A Amwaj favourite for sunset drinks and Greek-Mediterranean cuisine. The rooftop terrace offers panoramic views of the lagoon and is particularly busy on weekend evenings. Best visited from 5pm onwards.
- **K妨餐厅** — Authentic Asian cuisine with a focus on Thai and Chinese dishes. Casual atmosphere with outdoor seating.
- **Nama** — Contemporary Bahraini and Gulf cuisine. A local favourite for family dinners.

### Cafés and Light Bites

- **Baba Ha** — Popular Arabic coffee house with shisha. Open late, popular with groups.
- **Gloria Jean's** — International coffee chain with WiFi. Good option for remote workers.
- Various independent cafés dot the pedestrian promenade, offering specialty coffee, fresh juices, and light snacks.

## Beach Clubs and Pool Venues

Amwaj Islands is home to some of Bahrain's best beach clubs, particularly popular during the summer months (April to October).

### The Art Hotel Beach Club

The Art Hotel & Resort's private beach club is open to non-residents for a day pass. Features include:

- Private beach with sun loungers and parasols
- Large swimming pool with lagoon access
- Poolside bar and restaurant service
- Water sports including kayaking and paddle boarding

**Day pass:** Approximately BHD 15–25 depending on the season. Contact the hotel directly for current rates.

### Cavo Rooftop Pool

While primarily a restaurant and lounge, Cavo Rooftop also has a small rooftop pool area accessible to guests. It's more of a party venue than a traditional beach club, known for its DJ events and foam pool parties during summer.

### Public Beach Access

Amwaj Islands has several public beach areas accessible without charge, though facilities are limited. The lagoon side offers calmer waters suitable for swimming and paddle sports.

## Things to Do in Amwaj Islands

### Beach and Water Activities

- **Swimming and sunbathing** — The calm lagoon waters are ideal for swimming. The Art Hotel beach club offers the most comfortable beach experience with full facilities.
- **Paddle boarding and kayaking** — Available for rent at several points along the lagoon promenade.
- **Jet skiing and banana boat rides** — Available through beach vendors near the public beach areas (seasonal).

### The Amwaj Lagoon Promenade

The pedestrian walkway that circles the central lagoon is a highlight of Amwaj Islands. At approximately 2 kilometres long, it's perfect for an evening stroll with views across the water. The promenade is lined with cafés and benches, and is particularly lively on Thursday and Friday evenings when families and groups gather.

### Nearby Attractions

Amwaj Islands' proximity to Muharraq means several cultural attractions are a short drive away:

- **Muharraq Souq** — Traditional Bahraini market, 10 minutes by car. Great for local crafts, textiles, and fresh produce.
- **Bahrain National Museum** — 15 minutes from Amwaj. Offers insight into Bahrain's history and culture.
- **The Pearl Monument** — The iconic roundabout marking the entrance to the Manama financial district, 15 minutes away.
- **CityCentre Bahrain** — Large shopping mall with international brands, cinema, and food court, 10 minutes by car.

## Living in Amwaj Islands

Amwaj Islands is one of Bahrain's most desirable residential areas, particularly for expats. The island community features:

- **Upscale apartments and villas** — Rental prices vary widely; a one-bedroom apartment typically starts from BHD 350/month, with luxury options reaching BHD 800+.
- **International schools nearby** — The British School of Bahrain and several other options within easy reach.
- **Amenities** — Supermarkets, pharmacies, gyms, and salons are all within the island complex.

## Best Time to Visit

Amwaj Islands is most vibrant from **October to April**, when Bahrain's weather is mild and outdoor dining and beach activities are at their best. Summer months (May to September) see extreme heat, though beach clubs and indoor venues remain active.

**Peak times:** Thursday and Friday evenings are the busiest, when locals and residents flock to the restaurants and promenade. Saturday is also popular.

**Quiet times:** Weekday afternoons tend to be quieter, ideal for a relaxed lunch or peaceful beach visit.

## Nightlife in Amwaj Islands

Amwaj's nightlife is more laid-back compared to Juffair or Adliya, centred around restaurant lounges and rooftop venues rather than clubs. The standout venues for evening drinks include:

- **Cavo Rooftop** — The most popular evening destination, particularly for sunset drinks. DJ nights on weekends during summer.
- **The Art Hotel bars** — Multiple venues within the hotel complex, from the lobby lounge to the poolside bar.
- **Laguna Lounge** — A favourite for relaxed evening drinks with lagoon views.

Amwaj does not have dedicated nightclubs; those seeking clubbing should head to Juffair or Adliya.

## Practical Information

**Currency:** Bahrain Dinar (BHD). 1 BHD ≈ 2.65 USD. ATMS widely available.

**Dress code:** Casual by day. Smart casual in most restaurants and lounges in the evening. Beachwear is for the beach only.

** tipping:** 10–15% is standard in restaurants if service charge isn't included (check your bill).

**Languages:** Arabic is official. English is widely spoken throughout Amwaj's commercial areas.

**Getting around:** A car is useful but not essential. Taxis and ride-share are readily available.

**Contact:** For specific venue information, opening hours, and reservations, check the venue's social media pages or call ahead, especially during Ramadan and public holidays when hours may change.
